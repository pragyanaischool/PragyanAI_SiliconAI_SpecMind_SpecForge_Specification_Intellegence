================================================================================
SPECIFICATION: Quad-Mode SPI Master Controller (spi_master_top)
Revision: 3.0 | Protocol: Motorola SPI (CPOL 0/1, CPHA 0/1) | Domain: Serial Bus
================================================================================

1. MODULE OVERVIEW
A master-mode SPI controller supporting Motorola SPI modes 0, 1, 2, and 3. 
Includes an internal clock divider, 8-word TX/RX FIFOs, and 4 dedicated slave 
chip-select (CS_N) outputs with configurable setup/hold times.

2. PORTS & INTERFACES
Port Name       | Dir | Width  | Clock  | Polarity | Description
----------------|-----|--------|--------|----------|-----------------------------------------
clk             | in  | [0:0]  | clk    | edge     | System core clock (100MHz)
rst_n           | in  | [0:0]  | clk    | low      | Master asynchronous active-low reset
sclk            | out | [0:0]  | clk    | edge     | Generated SPI serial clock
mosi            | out | [0:0]  | clk    | high     | Master Out Slave In serial data
miso            | in  | [0:0]  | clk    | high     | Master In Slave Out serial data
cs_n            | out | [3:0]  | clk    | low      | Active-low chip selects for up to 4 slaves
cfg_cpol        | in  | [0:0]  | clk    | high     | Clock polarity (0 = idle low, 1 = idle high)
cfg_cpha        | in  | [0:0]  | clk    | high     | Clock phase (0 = sample lead, 1 = sample trail)
cfg_clk_div     | in  | [7:0]  | clk    | high     | SCLK divisor = clk / (2 * (cfg_clk_div + 1))
tx_data         | in  | [7:0]  | clk    | high     | Parallel byte to transmit
tx_valid        | in  | [0:0]  | clk    | high     | Write strobe into TX FIFO
tx_ready        | out | [0:0]  | clk    | high     | TX FIFO ready for next byte
rx_data         | out | [7:0]  | clk    | high     | Received byte from RX FIFO
rx_valid        | out | [0:0]  | clk    | high     | RX FIFO contains valid byte
rx_ready        | in  | [0:0]  | clk    | high     | Read acknowledge from core
spi_busy        | out | [0:0]  | clk    | high     | Asserted while shifting bits

3. STATE MACHINE
- IDLE       : cs_n = 4'b1111, sclk holds CPOL level. Transitions to ASSERT_CS on tx_valid.
- ASSERT_CS  : Drives target cs_n low. Waits for 1/2 SCLK period for setup.
- SHIFT_BITS : Shifts 8 data bits on MOSI, samples MISO according to CPOL/CPHA.
- DEASSERT_CS: Restores cs_n high. Waits hold margin before next transaction.

4. CORNER CASES
CC-01: CPHA=0 First Bit Drive
       First MOSI bit must be valid BEFORE the first SCLK edge when CPHA=0.
CC-02: RX FIFO Overflow
       Data received while RX FIFO is full must drop new byte and assert an rx_overflow error flag.
CC-03: Dynamic Divisor Modification
       Changing cfg_clk_div while spi_busy is high must be ignored until transaction completes.
